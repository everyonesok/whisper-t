# ADR-00XX: <short title, a noun phrase>

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What was true that forced a choice. Constraints, measurements, the thing that
made this a decision rather than an obvious default.

## Decision

What was chosen. One or two sentences.

## Consequences

What this costs. Include the bad parts — an ADR that only lists benefits is
marketing, and it won't help the person who later wonders why things are like this.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and why it lost. This is the section that stops a future
reader re-walking a path you already ruled out.
